# Code Of Conduct

Brawler uses a simple contribution standard:

- Be respectful and direct.
- Keep discussion focused on the work.
- Critique code, design, documentation, and decisions without personal attacks.
- Do not harass, threaten, or intentionally disrupt contributors or users.
- Do not post private information, secrets, credentials, tokens, personal data, or confidential operational details.

The maintainer may edit, hide, lock, or reject contributions and discussions that violate these rules.
