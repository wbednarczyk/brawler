# Naming Shortlist

This document captures the current naming shortlist for the application. Brawler remains the temporary repository codename until a separate rename decision is made and applied across product copy, packaging, code, and documentation.

## Active Candidate

### Noktar

Why it is on the shortlist:

- Short, readable, and strong without feeling overly aggressive.
- Fits the first logo direction: dark radar, sweep, signal lock, and market terminal.
- Fits the visual palette: near-black/navy background with cyan, blue, purple, and magenta glow.
- Carries a useful accidental meaning: in Ido, `noktar` means "to be night".
- Quick search did not show an obvious active software, finance, or app product named exactly `Noktar`.

Product associations:

- Night signal desk.
- Radar sweep through market noise.
- Focused instrument for finding company-specific information.
- Watchlist monitoring without implying trading advice.

Open checks before final selection:

- Domain availability.
- App store and package name availability.
- GitHub organization/repository availability.
- EUIPO, USPTO, and Polish trademark searches.
- Final user-facing pronunciation and logo lockup test.

## Removed Or Parked

Previously explored names were removed or parked due to collision risk, weaker fit, awkward sound, or moving away from the desired product feel. The active shortlist is intentionally reduced to `Noktar` only.
