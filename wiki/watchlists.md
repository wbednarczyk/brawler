# Watchlists

A watchlist is a group of companies from your library. Today, Inbox and Report
Season can be filtered by a list, so a list is how you say "these are the ones I
care about this quarter". A company can be on several lists.

## The screen

- **Left**: your lists with their member counts. Pick one.
- **Right**: the selected list — its name, `Rename` / `Remove`, and one filled
  action, **Add companies**. Below it every member: ticker, name (ISIN on wide
  windows), **Open company** (takes you to the company's Spółka screen) and
  **Remove from list** (an ✕ with its label; in a narrow window the label hides
  but the button keeps its name for screen readers).
- On a narrow window only the names and counts show; tap a list to open it as
  its own view, with `Back to lists` at the top to return.

## Everyday moves

- **Create a list** — type a name in the header field, `Create`. Creating is
  rare, so the button is quiet; adding companies is the highlighted action.
- **Add companies** — opens a picker over your library: search, tick, `Add
  selected · n`. Companies already on the list are shown greyed with a note.
- **Remove a company** — ✕ on its row. Nothing is deleted from the app; the
  company just leaves this list.
- **Remove a list** — asks for an inline confirmation first (a list with
  eighteen companies is work, not a click).

## Empty states tell you the next step

No lists yet → *Create the first list*. An empty list → *Add companies*. A
search with no match → create a list with the name you typed.
