# Composable cockpit views

A **view** is your own multi-panel dashboard: a grid of resizable, dockable
panels you pick and arrange yourself — feed, claims, fundamentals, and more,
side by side instead of one at a time. Build as many as you want, save them
by name, and jump between them from the sidebar.

## Creating a view

In the sidebar, below the built-in modes, click **"+ New view"**:

1. **Name it** — required up front.
2. **Pick a grid.** Click a quick preset — **2×2**, **2×3**, or **3×3** — or
   choose a **custom size**: column/row sliders and number inputs are linked
   both ways (drag the slider or type a number, either updates the other),
   with a live preview of the grid as you adjust it.
3. An **empty grid** appears, one **"Pick a panel"** button per cell. Click a
   cell and choose what goes there.

The view is saved as soon as you create it, and shows up as its own entry in
the sidebar, under the built-in modes, right away.

## Panels available

The panel picker offers: **Feed**, **Inspector**, **Fundamentals**,
**Claims**, **Quality**, **Report comparison**, **Report documents**, and
**Notebook** — plus the library screens (Watchlists, Research, Events, Report
Season) if you want them pinned into a view instead of reached from the
sidebar. Panels that are tied to a company (Claims, Report comparison) show
the company name in their tab once you've selected one via Feed or Inspector.

## Rearranging, and saving changes

Add, remove, move, or resize panels freely at any time — nothing is fixed
once the grid exists. These changes are **not** saved automatically: use the
**Save layout** button in the toolbar to persist the arrangement (retype the
view's name in the field next to it, since it isn't pre-filled). Until you
save, your rearranging only lasts for the current session.

## Switching and deleting views

Click a saved view's entry in the sidebar to open it. To delete one, hover
over its row to reveal a delete icon and confirm — this only removes the
saved layout, never any of your data.

## Opening a company straight into its dashboard

From **Companies**, clicking a company doesn't open a blank view — it opens
a **curated dashboard** for that company: a calm starting set of panels
(Fundamentals, Feed, Claims, Quality, Report documents, Notebook) already in
place. It's a view like any other, so you can add, remove, or move panels the
same way; use **Save dashboard** to keep your changes for that company. The
same entry points (a pinned sidebar company, a feed item, or a Today
**Review** button) land you on the same dashboard.
