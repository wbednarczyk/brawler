# Report Season

A per-company checklist for the run-up to each quarterly/annual report:
what's still open, what you expect, and — once the numbers land — how they
compared.

## Where

Library nav → **Report Season**, or command palette → **Open screen:
Report Season**.

## The screen

- **Scope**: filter by watchlist, or **All watchlists**.
- **Upcoming reports**: every tracked company with a report date ahead,
  soonest first. A status chip shows where you are: **Nadchodzący**
  (nothing done yet), **Przygotowany** (prepared), **Przejrzany**
  (reviewed).
- **Past reports**: the same list, after the report date.
- Open a row for its **pre-report card**:
  - **Mark as prepared** / **Mark as reviewed** — your own checklist state
    for this occurrence.
  - **Company** / **Claims** — jump to the company or its open theses.
  - **Open research questions** and **unresolved claims** due around the
    report.
  - **Expectations** — see below.
  - Last-period KPIs and recent evidence, for context while you prepare.

## Pre-report expectations

Write down what you expect before the report lands, then let the actual
numbers answer once they're confirmed. Full write-up:
[The decision journal and pre-report expectations](decision-journal.md).

## Everyday moves

- **See what needs prep this week** — scope to a watchlist, scan the
  upcoming list.
- **Record your call before the report** — open a card, **Add
  expectations** (or **Edit expectations** if you already wrote one — it
  stays editable until the report lands).
- **Check your call after the report** — the card shows expected vs. actual
  automatically once facts are confirmed; add a verdict note with **Save
  verdict**.
- **A card failed to load** — **Refresh card** retries just that one.
