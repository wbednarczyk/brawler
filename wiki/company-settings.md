# Per-company settings

Some settings apply to one company at a time — how it's tracked, not what's
in it. Brawler gives you two ways to change them: a quick edit while you're
already looking at a company, and a bulk surface for changing several
companies at once.

## Quick edit, one company at a time

Open the company (Spółka screen) → **Open fundamentals**. From there you can
set:

- **Autopilot mode** — off / assist / autopilot (see
  [Autopilot](autopilot.md) for what each mode does).
- **IR reports page URL** — the company's investor-relations page, used when
  Brawler looks for a report to fetch.

## Bulk edit, many companies at once

Go to **Companies → Manage settings**. This swaps the company list for a
two-pane surface:

1. **Pick companies** on the left — check them individually, **select all**,
   or **select everyone in a watchlist** to scope the change to that group.
2. **Set the value** on the right, grouped by topic (the **Automation** group
   holds autopilot mode). Whatever you set applies to every company you
   picked.

If the selected companies don't all share the same value for a setting, it
shows as mixed rather than silently picking one — nothing is overwritten
until you explicitly choose a value.

This is the place to switch a whole watchlist to **assist** mode in one go,
instead of opening each company individually.
