# The decision journal and pre-report expectations

Brawler can now capture your **judgment** — what you decided about a company
and what you expected before a report — so you can look back later and see how
your thinking actually held up. Both features are factual mirrors: the app
records and replays what you wrote, it never grades you.

## The decision journal

A per-company, append-only log of your investment decisions.

**Where:** open the company (the [Spółka screen](company-view.md)) →
workshop bar → **Open decision journal**. A cross-company view lives in the
command palette (`Ctrl+K`) as **Open screen: Decision journal**.

**Writing an entry:**

1. Pick the decision kind — **buy**, **pass**, **keep watching**, or
   **sell note**.
2. Set the decision date (it can differ from today — backfill an older
   decision honestly).
3. Write your rationale in Markdown.
4. Optionally attach **evidence links** — a report, a note, a claim, a quality
   assessment — so the entry stays anchored to what you actually knew.

**Entries are immutable.** There is no edit and no delete — the database
itself refuses both. If your view changes, write a **follow-up entry** that
supersedes the old one; the journal shows the chain. That is the point: a
journal you can quietly rewrite is worthless for judging your own judgment.

## Pre-report expectations

Before a company reports, write down what you expect — then let the actual
numbers answer.

**Where:** the **Report Season** screen (Library nav, or command palette →
**Open screen: Report Season**) → a company's upcoming-report card →
**Add expectations**.

1. Record your overall **stance** for the report (a short free-text take).
2. Optionally add **metric rows** — revenue, net profit, or any tracked KPI —
   each with your expected value or range.
3. Save. You can revise freely **until the period's facts are confirmed**.

When the report arrives and its facts are confirmed in Brawler, the
expectation **freezes** — it becomes read-only, exactly as written. The card
then shows an **expected vs. actual** review: your numbers next to the
reported ones, side by side. You can attach a **resolution note** — what you
learned — which becomes part of the record.

## What this is not

No scoring, no accuracy percentage, no "you were wrong" badge. Brawler shows
you the record and leaves the judgment about your judgment to you. (As
everywhere in the app: decision support, never buy/sell advice.)
